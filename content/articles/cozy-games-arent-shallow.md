Every few months a thinkpiece declares cozy games a dead end — pleasant, profitable, and artistically weightless. The critique assumes stakes equal depth. It isn't so.

## The contract

A [cozy games list](/games/cozy-games/) offers something the rest of the medium stopped guaranteeing: you will not be ambushed. No jump scare, no forced grind, no cliffhanger you didn't consent to. That safety is the design, not a lack of ambition.

## Depth without pressure

A farming loop or a gentle town sim asks for attention the way a [slow cinema film](/film/slow-cinema-patience/) does — through repetition and small change, not spectacle. The player sets the pace. That is a harder design problem than a boss fight, not an easier one.

## Why it fits a mood-first library

Cozy games sit naturally beside a [rainy-window record](/music/classical-rainy-window/) or a [lofi focus list](/music/late-night-focus-lofi/). All three keep the same promise: the work waits for you. In a media diet full of demands, that restraint is the feature.

Cozy isn't the absence of stakes. It's the presence of consent.
